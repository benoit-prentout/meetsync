# Privacy Policy

**MeetSync** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to third parties.

## What data does the extension access?

- **OAuth email** – used only to authenticate with your own Apps Script backend.
- **Google Meet notes** – read from your Google Drive and written to your own master Google Doc. All data stays within your Google Workspace.

## Where does data go?

**Nowhere outside your Google account.** The extension communicates exclusively with your own Apps Script web app, which runs under your identity and writes to your own Google Docs. No data is sent to any external server or third party.

## How can you revoke access?

1. Go to your [Google Account Permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
2. Remove the MeetSync extension.
3. In Chrome, go to `chrome://extensions`, find MeetSync, and click **Remove**.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the version in this repository will be updated.
